Important:

Do NOT invent specific prices, ingredients, opening hours, awards, chef names, history facts, or menu items that were not provided. Where information is unavailable, use sensible placeholders or sections that can be edited later.

Website goal:

Create a professional restaurant website that helps visitors:

1. Discover the restaurant and its atmosphere.

2. View the menu.

3. Learn about the restaurant.

4. See popular dishes and food photography.

5. Make a reservation or contact the restaurant.

6. Get directions to the restaurant.

7. Easily call or message the restaurant.

8. View delivery/takeout information.

9. Read selected customer testimonials/reviews.

Design direction:

- Elegant classic Chinese restaurant aesthetic.

- Inspired by an established, nostalgic Karachi restaurant rather than a trendy startup.

- Warm, sophisticated and inviting.

- Use a tasteful combination of deep red, dark charcoal/black, warm cream and subtle gold accents.

- Use elegant typography with strong readability.

- Add subtle Chinese-inspired visual details, but do not make the design look stereotypical or overly decorative.

- Use high-quality food photography with large appetizing images.

- Use generous spacing and a premium restaurant layout.

- Avoid excessive animations.

- Add subtle hover effects and smooth scrolling.

- The website must feel trustworthy and suitable for families, couples, groups and long-time customers.
